# Sidenote 0.1.0-dev.2

The second public preview of Sidenote for Windows, on the Dev channel.

## Install and update

If you installed dev.1 and stayed on the Dev channel, Sidenote checks the Dev feed and downloads the update in the background. It updates your existing install. When it's ready, choose **Install and restart**, or keep working and let Sidenote try the update the next time you open it. Finish any recording or background jobs before installing.

If you're new to Sidenote, download `sidenote-dev-Setup.exe` from this release. Optional: before you run it, check it against SHA256SUMS from this release. Then run it.

You'll need Hex for Windows to record and transcribe meetings. For notes and Ask, sign in to Claude Code or Codex and choose it in Settings. See the README's "What you need" for other options.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## In this build

- The sidebar has a new Sidenote logo and workspace row. Use its **New** menu to start a meeting.
- Home now opens to your daily brief and task cards. The Meetings list no longer has a Today strip.
- The Meetings toolbar has view tabs, search and a filter. Meetings are grouped by day, and rows show a short summary, people and outcome counts.
- The **This week** rail shows your meeting count, recorded hours and open tasks for the week.
- Meeting pages have a new header and cards for questions and tasks in the rail.
- The Ask bar is available on every page. If you follow a citation, **Back** returns you to the page where you asked the question.
- Settings pages are grouped by topic. The Tasks settings let you pick the starting view, how tasks are grouped and which rows to show.
- The Tasks page keeps grouping by due date after a restart while Projects is off. Turn Projects back on to restore your saved Project grouping.
- Setup checks the meeting listener before it changes anything. If an older install's listener can't be reused safely, Setup stops with a message and leaves your install as it was.
- Setup installs the Visual C++ runtime the speech engine needs when it's missing.

## Known gaps

- Use **Start** and **Stop** to record a meeting. Sidenote does not detect meeting boundaries.
- Notes are unavailable for transcripts over the limit of about 250,000 characters.
- Olive is the only colour theme you can use. A colour picker is still pending.
- The warning banner styling and the Ask bar height and menu will change in dev.3.
- Workspace and team filters come later.
- Setup can't yet take over the meeting listener from an older Sidenote install in a different folder. Uninstall the older copy first. Updates from dev.1 are not affected.
- The installer and app are unsigned.
- CI still has no speed or smoothness budgets. A full 24-hour idle soak is pending for this build.
